  • Donations to Strays Home are voluntary gifts made to support animal welfare work.
  • Donations made by bank transfer go directly to our bank account. Donations made through third-party platforms such as GoFundMe are subject to those platforms' own terms, fees and policies.
  • Unless you have agreed a specific restriction with us in writing, we allocate donations to wherever the need is greatest. If the specific animal or case you named is already funded, we will apply your donation to the next animal in greatest need.

3. Adoption

  • Submitting an adoption enquiry does not reserve an animal or create any obligation on either side.
  • We assess every applicant and may decline any application, at our discretion and without giving reasons, where we believe it is not in the animal's best interest.
  • Adopters are asked to sign an adoption agreement. Animals must not be sold, used for breeding, guarding, fighting, or given away to a third party. If an adoption does not work out, the animal must be returned to us.
  • We share the health and behavioural information known to us in good faith, but we cannot guarantee an animal's future health, temperament or behaviour. Rescued animals may have unknown histories.
  • Once an animal is adopted, its care, safety and any resulting costs or liabilities become the adopter's responsibility.

  • You are responsible for your own actions when handling or transporting an animal. Seek medical attention immediately if you are bitten or scratched.

  • Volunteers must follow our safety instructions and animal-handling guidance on site, and must be 16 or over unless accompanied by a parent or guardian.
