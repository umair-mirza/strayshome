📞 Get in touch

Contact us or report a stray animal in Islamabad

Talk to us. Whether you've found an animal in trouble anywhere in Islamabad or Rawalpindi, want to adopt, want to help, or just want to check we're real before you donate — we'd rather you asked.

🚨 Animal emergency?

Call first — don't email. We answer the phone faster than anything else. If we can't pick up, send a WhatsApp with a photo and a dropped location pin.

Rescue line: +92 311 5395616 Emergency: +92 333 3499782 WhatsApp us

We're a small volunteer team and can't reach every animal, particularly outside Islamabad and Rawalpindi. If we can't come, we'll try to point you to someone who can. Read our first-aid guidance before moving an animal.

When we're reachable

  • Rescue line: daily, 8:00 am – 10:00 pm
  • Emergencies: any time, day or night
  • Visits & admin: by appointment

We are volunteers with day jobs. If we miss you, we will call back.

A word on scams

The only accounts we use are published on our donate page and our Instagram profile. We will never ask you for your card details, PIN or an OTP. If in doubt, call us before sending anything.
