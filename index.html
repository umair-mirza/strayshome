The reality on our roads

Pakistan's streets are full of animals nobody counts.

Hit by cars. Poisoned. Dumped in sacks. Starving through winter. There is no ambulance for them and no hospital that will take them in. There is only whoever decides to stop.

🚑

Emergency rescue

We answer calls from ordinary people who find an animal in trouble, then get to them as fast as our fuel and vehicles allow.

💊

Real medical care

Wound care, surgeries, drips, deworming, vaccinations and long recoveries — not a bandage and a prayer.

🏡

A home, not a cage

Animals stay with us for as long as they need. Healthy, friendly ones go to screened, permanent families.

We don't run on goodwill. We run on rupees.

Straight answers

Before you give, you deserve to know

Where does my money actually go?

Veterinary treatment and surgeries, medicines, animal feed, fuel for rescue runs, shelter upkeep and the day-to-day cost of keeping over a hundred animals alive. We are volunteer-run, so there are no salaries drawn for leadership.

Can I donate to an animal shelter in Pakistan from abroad?

Yes. Our GoFundMe campaign accepts international cards, and our Meezan Bank IBAN works for international wire transfers. Full details are on the donate page.

Can I see the animal I helped?

Absolutely. Message us on WhatsApp or Instagram with your donation reference and we'll share updates and photos of who your money helped. Visits to the sanctuary can be arranged by appointment.

I found an injured stray animal in Islamabad. What do I do?

Call our rescue line on +92 311 5395616. Keep the animal calm, don't force food or water, and send us a photo and exact location on WhatsApp if you can. There are first-aid steps here.

Which areas of Pakistan does Strays Home cover?

Our sanctuary is at Pind Sangrial, Shah Allah Ditta, Islamabad, and we respond to rescue calls across Islamabad and Rawalpindi. We also advise callers elsewhere in Pakistan and help arrange adoptions nationwide where a safe transport plan exists.
