Rescue needs more than money. It needs drivers, feeders, fosters, photographers, designers, spreadsheet people and anyone with a spare Saturday in Islamabad or Rawalpindi. If you've been looking for a way to be useful — this is it.

Before you sign up

Volunteers must be 16+ (or accompanied by a guardian), and shelter work means dust, noise, and animals that may be frightened. It is not glamorous. It is, however, the most worthwhile way we know to spend a Saturday.

Foster

Lend a room. Save a life.

Fostering means keeping a rescued animal in your home temporarily — usually two to eight weeks — while it recovers or waits for adoption. Every foster place frees a space at the sanctuary for the next emergency.

We provide: food, medication, veterinary cover and round-the-clock advice. You provide: a safe indoor space, time and patience.

Fosters are needed most for orphaned kittens and puppies, post-operative recovery cases, and dogs who simply don't cope with kennel life.
