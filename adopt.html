The process

Five steps, roughly a week

We take our time because a failed adoption is devastating for an animal that has already been abandoned once.

Tell us about you

Fill in the form below or message us. Household, space, other pets, working hours, experience.

We match you

We suggest animals that genuinely suit your life — which may not be the one you first picked.

Meet in person

Come to the sanctuary by appointment and spend real time with the animal.

Home check

A quick visit or video walkthrough to confirm the space is safe and secure.

Go home together

You sign a simple adoption agreement, take the medical records, and we stay in touch.

✅ We're looking for adopters who

  • Will keep the animal indoors or in a securely fenced space
  • Understand a rescue may need weeks to settle and trust
  • Can afford routine food and veterinary care
  • Have agreement from everyone living in the home
  • See this as a 10–15 year commitment, not a phase

🚫 We won't adopt out for

  • Guarding, breeding, fighting or resale — ever
  • Chaining or permanent rooftop/balcony confinement
  • Gifts or surprises for someone who hasn't agreed
  • Households where a previous pet was abandoned or given away

We reserve the right to decline any application without giving a reason. The animal's safety comes first.

Common questions

Adoption FAQs

Does adoption cost anything?

No. Adoption is free. If you'd like to make a donation towards the animal's past medical care you're very welcome to, but it is never a condition.

Can I adopt if I live outside Islamabad?

Often yes, within Pakistan, provided we can complete a home check (in person or by video) and arrange safe transport. Tell us your city in the form.

Are your dogs "pedigree" breeds?

Mostly they are Pakistani street dogs and mixes — and they are extraordinary. Occasionally we rescue an abandoned breed dog too. We match on temperament, not appearance.

What if it doesn't work out?

Talk to us first — most problems in the first month are normal settling issues we can help with. If it genuinely can't work, the animal comes back to us. Never rehome or release them yourself.

Can I adopt a cat?

Yes. We rescue and rehome cats and kittens as well as dogs. Cats are usually adopted as indoor-only.
