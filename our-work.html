Six programmes, one goal

Our stray animal rescue & shelter work in Pakistan

Rescue is the easy part. Picking an animal up off the road takes an hour. Getting it healthy, safe and permanently home takes months — and money. Here is exactly what we do across Islamabad and Rawalpindi, and what it costs.

What we do

Our programmes

🚑

Emergency rescue

We respond to reports of animals hit by vehicles, injured, poisoned, trapped, abandoned or abused. A volunteer travels out, stabilises the animal and transports it to veterinary care.

Typical cost: Rs 2,000–6,000 per rescue in fuel, transport and first response.

💊

Veterinary treatment

Wound management, fracture repair, amputations, tick fever and parvo treatment, drips, deworming and long courses of medication — plus the weeks of nursing that follow.

Typical cost: Rs 5,000–25,000 per animal depending on severity.

🍖

Daily feeding rounds

Community dogs and cats that don't need rescuing still need to eat — especially through winter. Consistent feeding also keeps animals healthy and easier to catch for sterilisation.

Typical cost: Feed is our single largest recurring expense.

💉

Sterilise, vaccinate, return

The only humane and effective way to control street animal populations and rabies risk. Animals are neutered, vaccinated, ear-notched for identification and returned to their own territory.

Typical cost: Rs 6,000–15,000 per animal including anti-rabies vaccination.

🏡

Sanctuary & rehabilitation

Animals too injured, elderly or traumatised to return to the street live at our sanctuary in Shah Allah Ditta, with food, shelter, ongoing medication and human company.

Typical cost: Ongoing, every single day, for life.

📣

Awareness & education

Talking to schools, neighbourhoods and online audiences about humane treatment, why culling fails, why sterilisation works, and what to do when you find an animal in distress.

Typical cost: Mostly volunteer time — and the most valuable thing we do long-term.

What to do before you call us

A few minutes of the right first aid can be the difference between a rescue and a recovery of a body. Please read this before you move the animal.

Call +92 311 5395616 Send photo on WhatsApp

Please note: we are a small volunteer team and cannot reach every animal, especially outside Islamabad and Rawalpindi. If we can't come, we will try to guide you to someone who can.

Keep your distance first

A frightened, injured animal may bite. Approach slowly, speak softly, and don't corner it against traffic.

Don't force food or water

An animal in shock or with internal injuries can choke. Offer water nearby and let them choose.

Move it out of danger — carefully

If it's safe for you, slide the animal onto a blanket or flat board. Support the spine. Never pull a limb.

Send us a photo and exact location

A WhatsApp photo plus a dropped pin saves us the most precious thing we have: time.

Where the money goes

An honest look at our costs

These are the four things that consume almost everything we raise.

Veterinary

Surgeries, consultations, diagnostics, medication and post-operative care.

Food

Dry feed, rice and chicken for over a hundred animals, every single day.

Fuel & transport

Reaching rescues, running feeding routes and getting animals to the vet.

Shelter upkeep

Kennels, fencing, bedding, cleaning supplies, water and repairs.
